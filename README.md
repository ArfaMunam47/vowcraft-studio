## 🌸 What Is VowCraft?

**VowCraft** is an actively developed wedding invitation creation platform with a mission to make beautiful, personalised digital wedding invitations accessible to everyone, regardless of their graphic design experience.

Imagine creating a wedding invitation that feels like it was designed by a professional, with elegant typography, rich colours, delicate ornaments, personalised photographs, cinematic animations, and beautiful music, without needing to hire a designer or learn complicated design software.

That is the experience VowCraft aims to make possible.

The goal is not simply to provide another collection of templates. It is to build an intuitive creative platform where people can express their stories, celebrate meaningful moments, and create invitations that feel uniquely theirs. ♡

> 💌 Your story. Your style. Your invitation.

## 🎨 The Inspiration Behind VowCraft

The idea began with a simple observation: people want wedding invitations that feel special, personal, and memorable, but creating a sophisticated design can be difficult without professional design skills.

Inspired by the beautiful, cinematic wedding invitation designs discovered on Pinterest, VowCraft aims to bridge the gap between creative inspiration and accessible design.

A wedding invitation can be much more than a static image. It can unfold through elegant scenes, reveal names through beautiful typography, showcase photographs, feature ornamental details, and bring the entire experience together with music and motion.

**Our vision is to make this level of creativity accessible to ordinary people.**

VowCraft takes inspiration from a broader creative possibility, not from copying individual designers' work. The platform aims to develop original templates and an intuitive editing experience built around the needs of its users.

## 🎯 Our Mission

To empower everyone to create beautiful, personalised, and memorable digital wedding invitations without requiring professional design expertise.

We want to make creative tools easier to use, help people celebrate their special moments in their own style, and explore new opportunities for independent creators to turn their creativity into a skill.

Our guiding principle is simple:

**Technology should make creativity more accessible, not more complicated.**

## ✨ What We Aim to Build

The following capabilities represent the product vision. They will be implemented and tested incrementally throughout development.

### 💌 Personalised Wedding Invitations

Customise invitation text, names, wedding dates, venues, colours, typography, layouts, and decorative details to create a design that reflects your personal taste.

### 🎀 A Growing Collection of Templates

Explore a diverse collection of original wedding invitation templates across elegant, traditional, contemporary, floral, minimalist, and cinematic styles.

The long-term ambition is to build a catalogue of **1,000+ templates**, with meaningful variety rather than repetitive designs.

### 🎬 Cinematic Video Invitations

Transform static invitation designs into beautifully animated video experiences featuring elegant transitions, scene changes, animated typography, ornamental effects, and carefully timed visual details.

The goal is to enable users to preview and export complete invitation videos for digital sharing.

### 🖼️ Personal Photographs and Decorative Elements

Upload personal photographs and customise visual details using decorative frames, floral arrangements, wedding motifs, borders, badges, ornaments, and other design elements.

### 🎵 Music and Sound

Add suitable background music, control volume, and coordinate audio with the invitation's animation and scene timing.

Video exports must combine audio and visuals correctly, producing an actual shareable video rather than just an animated browser preview.

### 🌍 Cultural and Creative Diversity

The initial design direction includes Pakistani and South Asian weddings, with celebrations such as Mehndi, Barat, Walima, and Nikah.

Support for Urdu and English typography, additional wedding traditions, and diverse visual styles will be developed as the product evolves.

### 💡 Accessible to Beginners

The platform is intended for people who have never used professional design software.

Users should be able to select a template, personalise it, preview the result, and eventually export their invitation through a straightforward and intuitive workflow.

### 💸 Opportunities for Independent Creators

A future ambition is to explore ways for aspiring designers, students, and independent creators to offer invitation-making services or sell original templates.

Creator profiles, customer orders, template sales, and marketplace features will be evaluated after the core invitation creation experience has been established.

## 🛠️ Technology and Development

VowCraft is being developed with an AI-assisted workflow while maintaining a focus on understandable, maintainable software.

**Development tools and planned technical foundation**

- ⚛️ React
- 🔷 TypeScript
- 🎨 Tailwind CSS
- 🤖 Google AI Studio for AI-assisted development
- 🐙 GitHub for source control and collaboration

Additional technologies and backend services will be introduced when they are required by the product and can be evaluated appropriately.

The architecture will evolve alongside the application. Technology choices will be guided by maintainability, security, performance, and actual product requirements.

AI assistance is part of the development process, but every important feature must still be implemented, tested, and reviewed.

## 🗺️ Development Roadmap

VowCraft is being built step by step. Each milestone must be tested and reviewed before the next stage begins.

- [ ] **01 · Product Foundation** — Landing page, application shell, initial template gallery, and design system.
- [ ] **02 · Template Selection and Editor** — Select a template and begin personalising it through a working editing interface.
- [ ] **03 · Personalisation Tools** — Text, typography, colours, layouts, decorative elements, badges, and photograph uploads.
- [ ] **04 · Animation and Scenes** — Scene-based invitations, transitions, timing controls, and playback.
- [ ] **05 · Music and Video Export** — Audio controls and genuine video rendering and downloading.
- [ ] **06 · Accounts and Saved Projects** — Authentication, project persistence, and secure backend integration.
- [ ] **07 · Production Readiness** — Security reviews, accessibility, performance, reliability, and deployment.
- [ ] **08 · Catalogue and Creator Opportunities** — Expand the template collection and evaluate creator-focused features.

*Roadmap items describe planned milestones. Their completion will be reflected here only after the corresponding functionality has been implemented and tested.*

## 🔐 Security, Privacy, and Quality

A real product must be more than visually impressive. It must also be reliable and safe to use.

As the platform develops, we will address:

- 🔒 Appropriate authentication and access controls.
- 🖼️ Secure handling and validation of uploaded files.
- 🗂️ Privacy and permissions for saved projects and personal content.
- 🔑 Safe management of credentials and API keys.
- ⚡ Performance and responsive behaviour across devices.
- ♿ Accessibility and keyboard usability.
- 📜 Appropriate licensing for fonts, images, templates, and audio.
- 🧪 Testing of critical workflows, including project saving and video export.

These responsibilities will be addressed throughout development, with particular attention to features that handle user data and uploaded content.

## 🚧 Current Development Status

**Status: Active Development**

VowCraft is currently focused on establishing the product foundation and developing its core capabilities incrementally.

The complete platform is not yet finished. Advanced editing, animation, video rendering, audio integration, user accounts, persistent storage, and creator marketplace features remain planned until implemented and verified.

We are taking a deliberate approach: build a feature, test it, review it, and then move forward.

The long-term goal is to develop VowCraft into a useful, dependable application that real people can use to create and share their wedding invitations.
